The Apology

I know. I know.
I know.

There is absolutely no excuse. I checked. I Googled "valid reasons to forget someone's birthday" and the results were: zero. Zilch. A complete and utter void.

And of all the birthdays to forget — it had to be yours. The one person who, the second I say "I think I have a fever," switches into the most attentive, terrifyingly competent almost-doctor I've ever had the privilege of annoying. I forgot the date. I have never once forgotten you.

The thing about almost having someone is that it makes you reckless. You think: there's time. She'll be there tomorrow. You forget that tomorrow sometimes lands on the 6th of May, and that the 6th of May doesn't wait for anyone — including idiots who should've known better. I should've known better.
Not from any movie. Just the truth, dressed up a little.

Exhibit A — Why This Is A Bigger Deal Than It Sounds

The case for Rose.

Medical Student  ·  5'1 of Pure Chaos  ·  Certified Force of Nature

She is a medical student — which means while everyone else is figuring out how to adult, she's busy memorising the entire human body and somehow still finding time to be effortlessly cool about it.

She's 5'1. Five feet and one inch. And yet she has the gravitational pull of something much, much larger. You feel her in every room she walks into. You feel her in rooms she isn't in too — that's the part nobody warns you about.

Her hair. Her lips. The colour of her hair specifically. There are people who spend hours trying to look like she just woke up.

01
When she gets angry, it is one of the most entertaining and mildly terrifying spectacles I've witnessed. I always end up smiling. Which probably makes it worse.
02
The second I say I'm hurt, something switches in her. Calm. Certain. Like she was made for it — which, technically, she is.
03
Her arguments are better than most people's good moods. Specific. Precise. I'd take a fight with her over small talk with anyone else, any day.
04
She's the kind of person you realise — too late — you should have held onto from the start. I realise it now. Later than I should have.
